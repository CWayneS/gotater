Doom for TaterOS
================

Two separate pieces live in this folder, under two licenses.

1. The engine: doom.js + doom.wasm
----------------------------------
A WebAssembly build of Chocolate Doom, from Cloudflare's doom-wasm port.

  Upstream source: https://github.com/cloudflare/doom-wasm
  Built from commit: 65e0d3ae2ffa604155eebd96ed40da6567bd08f4
  Our changes: doom-wasm-tateros.patch (in this folder). It updates the
    Emscripten flags for a current toolchain, links IDBFS for saves, and
    fixes `boolean` being two different sizes across files with newer
    Emscripten headers.
  Toolchain: Emscripten 3.1.74, `./scripts/build.sh`, then
    `wasm-opt --strip-debug -O2`.

Chocolate Doom and doom-wasm are free software under the GNU General Public
License, version 2 or later. See COPYING.md for the full text. The complete
corresponding source is the upstream commit above plus the patch.
